Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy describes how the mobile apps by Creative Island Software, developed by Bastián Berríos (Chile), handle information. It applies to the apps Kaizen, Toki, Danjiki and Bolsillo Claro, published on Google Play.

1. What the apps do (and don't do)

Our apps have no user accounts and no servers of our own. We do not collect, store or receive personal data on our own. The information you enter in the app (for example habits, study sessions, fasts, weight or expenses) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

On Android, if Google backup is turned on, the system may include the app's data in your backup and restore it on a new phone. Google manages that backup under its own policy.

2. Advertising with Google AdMob

The free Android versions show ads through Google AdMob (one ad when the app opens; we do not use banners). When doing so, the Google SDK may automatically collect and use, under its own policy:

  • The device's advertising ID.
  • App usage data and interactions with ads.
  • Device data (for example model, system version, language, IP address and approximate location) and diagnostic data.

This data is collected and managed by Google, not by Creative Island Software. Google uses it to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. In regions where the law requires it, the app shows a consent form (Google UMP) and respects your choice.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services.

3. Your choices and rights

  • Personalized ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device settings (Google settings → Ads).
  • Consent: if the app asked for it, you can change your choice from the in-app privacy option, when available.
  • Your local data: since it is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
  • Access, correction and deletion: since we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing to request from us; for the data Google manages, you can exercise your rights directly with Google.

4. Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this same page with its update date.
